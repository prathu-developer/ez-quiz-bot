Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 28 July 2026

Welcome to Ez Editorials. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information our Telegram Bot and Mini App collect, how it is used, and how it is protected.

To operate our services, we may collect:

  • Telegram User ID

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information may only be disclosed when required by applicable law or to comply with Telegram platform requirements.

4. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the services offered by Ez Editorials and to maintain community records and statistics.

Our services operate through Telegram and are hosted using trusted cloud providers. Your use of Telegram is also governed by Telegram's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration or misuse. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for users below the minimum age required by Telegram's Terms of Service.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page together with the revised "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact
